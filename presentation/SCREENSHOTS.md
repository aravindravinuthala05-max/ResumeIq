# Screenshot Checklist

Capture screenshots with a realistic resume and JD loaded. Keep the browser address bar out of the final slide image when possible, and make sure text is readable at presentation distance.

## 1. Landing Page

- **Visible:** ResumeIQ title, Analyze - Improve - Get Hired message, upload area, JD textarea, Analyze Resume button.
- **Why useful:** Establishes the product and entry point.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 1 or Slide 2.

## 2. Upload Screen

- **Visible:** Selected realistic resume filename and pasted software-engineering JD.
- **Why useful:** Shows the required inputs and simple workflow.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 5 or Slide 9.

## 3. ATS Score

- **Visible:** Circular score, status, ATS / JD Match heading, and score breakdown.
- **Why useful:** Demonstrates the existing weighted ATS result.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 11.

## 4. JD Matching

- **Visible:** Matched skills, Missing JD Skills, and the “Not currently supported by your resume” helper text.
- **Why useful:** Shows safe distinction between resume support and JD requirements.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 11 or Slide 12.

## 5. Strengths

- **Visible:** Strengths section with evidence-backed recommendation cards and evidence labels.
- **Why useful:** Shows what the resume already supports.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 12 or Slide 13.

## 6. Priority Improvements

- **Visible:** HIGH and MEDIUM recommendation cards with section, description, evidence, and JD relevance.
- **Why useful:** Demonstrates actionable advisor output.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 13.

## 7. Skills Intelligence

- **Visible:** Resume Skills, JD-Matched Skills, Missing JD Skills, and Evidence-Backed Skills groups.
- **Why useful:** Demonstrates the complete Skills Intelligence feature.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 12.

## 8. Resume Details

- **Visible:** Contact, Education, Experience, Projects, Certifications, Achievements, Languages, and Formatting sections.
- **Why useful:** Shows that detailed analyzer output was preserved.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 15.

## 9. Rewriter ORIGINAL/IMPROVED

- **Visible:** One rewrite card with exact Original text, Improved text, source section, factual-safety label, and copy button.
- **Why useful:** Demonstrates factual rewriting rather than text generation without controls.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 13.

## 10. Interview Preparation

- **Visible:** Numbered questions with category and metadata labels, including a JD Gap question.
- **Why useful:** Shows resume-grounded and safe JD-gap preparation.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 14.

## 11. Resume Health

- **Visible:** ATS Score, JD Match counts, Priority Improvements count, Rewrite Opportunities count after lazy loading, and Interview Preparation count.
- **Why useful:** Gives a concise report summary.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 15.

## 12. PDF Report

- **Visible:** Open PDF page with Resume Overview, ATS / JD Match, Strengths, Priority Improvements, Skills, and Interview Preparation headings.
- **Why useful:** Verifies the final downloadable deliverable.
- **PPT slide:** Slide 15 or Slide 16.

## Capture Notes

- Use the same resume and JD across screenshots for consistency.
- Include one unsupported JD skill so the safety wording is visible.
- Avoid showing raw session storage or JSON.
- Do not use screenshots with empty browser error pages.
- Keep one test-result terminal screenshot separately for Slide 16.
